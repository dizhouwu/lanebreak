    Move or attack. Target an enemy hero to draw nearby creep aggro. Target an allied creep to transfer aggro or deny it below 50% HP.

    The camera follows your hero by default. Press C to toggle free camera, or Space to recenter it on your hero. In free mode, pan with the screen edges, arrow keys, middle-mouse drag, or minimap.
