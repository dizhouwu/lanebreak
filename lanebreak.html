    Controls

    Left click
    Inspect a unit. Click a hero to see their equipment.
    Right click
    Move or attack. Target an enemy hero to draw nearby creep aggro. Target an allied creep to transfer aggro or deny it below 50% HP.
    C / Space
    The camera follows your hero by default. Press C to toggle free camera, or Space to recenter it on your hero. In free mode, pan with the screen edges, arrow keys, middle-mouse drag, or minimap.
    Q W E R
    Select a targeted ability, then click the battlefield to cast. Right-click or Esc cancels. Self-casts and Cairn's directional blasts trigger immediately.
    A / S
    Attack the target or attack-move to the cursor / stop and hold fire.
    1 through 6
    Activate the item in that inventory slot.
    B F T
    Shop, drink a potion, or recall to your fountain.
    P / Esc
    Pause solo play.
    G / M
    Cycle graphics quality / toggle sound.
    Neris spell recipes

    Choose three marks with Q, W, or E in any order. Press R to prepare the spell, then D or F to cast. Neris uses V for potions.

    Last-hit enemy creeps for gold. Destroy the outer tower, inner tower, and enemy core in order.
