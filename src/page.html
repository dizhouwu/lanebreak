    Controls

    Left click / drag
    Click to inspect or select a unit. Drag a box to select your hero and summons inside it.
    Z / X
    Select your hero or all units you control. F1 and F2 also work when the browser passes them through.
    Right click
    Move or attack with the selected units. Your hero can draw creep aggro by targeting a hero, or deny weakened allied creeps.
    C / Space
    The camera follows your hero by default. Press C to toggle free camera, or Space to recenter it on your hero. In free mode, pan with the screen edges, arrow keys, middle-mouse drag, or minimap. Use Fullscreen to keep the browser controls away from the play area.
    Q W E R
    Select a targeted ability, then click the battlefield to cast. Right-click or Esc cancels. Self-casts and Cairn's directional blasts trigger immediately.
    A / S
    Attack the target or attack-move to the cursor / stop and hold fire.
    1 through 6
    Activate the item in that inventory slot.
    B T
    Open the item shop or recall to your fountain.
    P / Esc
    Pause solo play.
    `
    Show or hide both teams, including hero levels and equipment.
    G / M
    Cycle graphics quality / toggle sound.
